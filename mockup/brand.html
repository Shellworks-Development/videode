Motion

Motion explains what changed. It never sits between you and the next keystroke: input is never delayed or blocked, and anything you drag follows the pointer 1:1. Use the switch at the top right to compare. Off makes everything instant. System follows the OS reduce-motion setting and is the default.

  • Short. 90–180ms for UI feedback and 300ms for the peek. Only the splash runs longer (under 1s), and you can skip it with any key.
  • One curve. Ease-out cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) for things arriving. Leaving is faster than arriving.
  • No motion on direct manipulation. Drag, trim, scrub and playback are never eased. Clips snap instantly; only the guide line flashes.
  • Off really means off. Every animation has an instant end state, so nothing is lost when motion is disabled. The playhead and meters still update; they are data, not decoration.

Settings window (Ctrl , / ⌘ ,)

Settings come first because animation needs a switch. They're saved as KDL in the platform config folder (~/.config/videode/settings.kdl, %APPDATA%\videode, ~/Library/Application Support/videode), are written whenever you change something, and take effect immediately.
